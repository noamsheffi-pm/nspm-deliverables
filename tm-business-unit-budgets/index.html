Traffic Management

Give every business unit its own budget

Traffic Management lets you cap each business unit's daily units and set its priority in one pipeline, so one app can't use the whole team quota.

See it in the design

One pipeline, one route per business unit

A router splits logs by business unit. Personalisation has its own cap of 650 units a day. What passes the cap goes to a second router: errors stay at High priority, the rest go to Low. Playback and ads keep their own routes.

The problem today

Many apps, one quota, caps built from alerts

One quota for many apps

All apps in a team share one daily quota. The first app to spike takes it from the others.

Caps live in alerts

To cap one app, you build a metric alert, a webhook that blocks it, and a second alert to unblock it. Any broken link and the app stays blocked or runs over.

Tiers are not budgets

Priority decides how data is stored, not how much each business unit may send. A per-tier quota cannot say "this unit gets 650 units a day".

Use case

Personalisation spends its own budget, and only its own

what happened

One app, one shared quota

Personalisation shares the team quota with every other app. Its daily limit is about 650 units, held today by alerts and a webhook.

what the page shows

A cap, then a priority

A router gives personalisation its own route. A Set quota caps it at 650 units a day and blocks the rest. A second router keeps errors at High and sends the rest to Low.

what the admin knows

Every unit is named

Each business unit has its own budget in the product, not in an alert chain. When a unit hits its cap, only that unit stops. Finance and the unit owner read the same number.
