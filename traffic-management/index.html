Traffic Management

One noisy business unit no longer spends everyone's quota

Traffic Management gives each business unit its own data pipeline and its own share of the company quota.

Design preview with sample data. The final product may differ. Hover the window to pause it; the corner button opens it larger.

The problem today

One business unit's spike blocks data for the others

One shared pool

The quota is set for the whole company, not per business unit. The one that spikes first uses what the others need.

Extra teams as a workaround

Companies split into more Coralogix teams just to get separate budgets. Queries across those teams then break.

Three screens, one decision

Routing, TCO and quota sit in different places. No one sees where data goes and what it costs in one view.

Use case

Cluster metrics stop eating the quota payments needs

What happened

The platform business unit runs the Kubernetes clusters, and their metrics grow every week. All business units share one metrics quota. When the cluster spikes, metrics from payments stop arriving.

What the page shows

The metrics pipeline. A route sends cluster metrics down their own path. A block drops debug metrics. A Set quota, "Platform share", lets 12 CxU a day through to platform. Payments keeps its own route.

What the admin knows

The spike stays inside the business unit that made it. Platform has its own share and its own data flow. No extra Coralogix team was created to get a separate budget.
