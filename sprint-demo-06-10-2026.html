Governance · Datasets
Historical Data ImportRolling Out
Why it matters

Customers import old data without blocking live data.

The import is measured beside the daily quota, not inside it, and it is billed once on uncompressed size.

Before

Live ingestion rejects anything older than 24 hours. A customer moving from another vendor had no way to bring their history with them.

→
Now

We copy the customer's files from their own S3 into a dedicated dataset on the Compliance tier. It can be queried and does not touch the daily quota.

Monetization · Quota
Monthly Team QuotaOpen to Early Adopters
Why it matters

Customers budget for the month, not for each day.

We are looking for early adopter accounts now. If a customer has busy weeks and quiet weeks, bring them to us.

Before

Quota was enforced per day. A team with one busy day hit the daily cap, even when the monthly budget still had room.

→
Now

A monthly plan per account. Teams use units across the month, and a daily burst limit stops one day from using the whole month.

What changed

What you can sell now

Four things customers could not do last month. You can offer all four today.
Three came from customer requests. The fourth brings TCO in line with Alerts and Parsing Rules.
Sell one usage page
Usage and quota for every team now sit on one page. Customers stop moving between two screens for one answer.
Sell TCO you can trust
The Log Policies table now shows the priority active right now. Customers see whether their quota policy has switched tiers.
Sell TCO as code
Every saved TCO policy now opens as Terraform or Kubernetes code. Customers copy UI policies straight into their IaC pipeline.
Sell historical import
Historical log data now imports into its own dataset from customer S3. Customers bring their history without blocking live data.
Say the claim, then stop and let them read. Names stay off the slide: the live policy status came from Kotak and Xplor cases, historical import from Collibra, CommIT and others. Historical import is run by our team in phase 1. View as Code is parity work. Caveats stay on the cards.
