The trade

What you give up, said plainly

No free overage graceThe room is now in the plan: 3 times your rate on any day, and a full month to even out.
No carry-overUnits you do not use in a month do not move to the next month.
If the month runs outYou get a warning first. If it runs out, new data stops until the 1st. Your account manager can add units before that. Pay-as-you-go is not part of the monthly plan at launch.
One cycleAll teams in your account share one cycle, from the 1st to the last day of the month.
