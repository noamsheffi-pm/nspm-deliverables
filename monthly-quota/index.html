What changes

Quiet days make room for busy days

daily rate × days

One total for the month

Your month total is your daily rate times the days in that month. 31 days in October, 30 in November.

up to 3×

Room for a busy day

Any one day can use up to 3 times your daily rate. A release or an incident day is not blocked at midnight.

1 month

Only the month has to hold

Quiet days leave units in the month for your busy workdays.

Good to know

How the month works in practice

Unused unitsUnits you do not use in a month do not carry over to the next month.
If the month runs outYou get a warning before the month total is used. If it runs out, new data stops until the 1st. Your account manager can add units before that.
One cycleAll teams in your account use the same cycle, from the 1st to the last day of the month.
Your usage viewUsage & Quota still shows usage per day, so you can see which days were busy.
