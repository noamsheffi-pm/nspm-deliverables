LiveAI Units: Monthly Cycle and Team Limit

Want to cap AI use per team? Set a monthly limit. It blocks at that number.

What it is

AI units run on a monthly cycle, with a limit for each team.

What you get

The number on the screen is the number that blocks. When a team reaches its monthly limit, AI stops for that team only.

Before

Enforced per day for the whole account. A team limit did not block.

Coming next · in build · no date

Two things we are building now

1Virtual Datasets

Many datasets, one name.

Define a virtual dataset once: its sources and one filter. Query it by one name in DataPrime and Explore. Your users stop joining datasets by hand.

Why

Teams split data into many datasets. Every query then lists them all, and a rename breaks saved queries.

Asked by

Via Transportation (PIPE), Gong.io, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Tango.me

2Data Pipelines

See how your data flows, and change it in one place.

A visual builder for routing, fan-out and transforms, from source to dataset. The new name for Traffic Management.

Why

Routing rules sit in TCO, parsing rules and dataset settings. Nobody sees the full path of a log.

Looking for

Design partners. Bring us an account that wants to try the builder early.

Say: two items, in this order. Both in build, no date. Virtual Datasets (CX-37168), priority 1: phase 1 = sources plus one DPXL filter; no aggregations or group by. The query is rejected if the user cannot read every source. Same-name syntax: transactions(virtual=false) reads the dataset itself. ⚠ Blocked: the DLI RFC (CX-41805) had no owner on 20-10; Citadel CX-37170 started its part. Do not give a date. PIPE: PIPEV2-4006 (Via Transportation, nine datasets unioned by hand, escalated by the TAM). Gong.io wants it for its dataset migration; Kotak asked in May. Data Pipelines (CX-49207), priority 2: renamed from Traffic Management on 02-11. Radiant (Anton, Naor) drafted the proto models, not blocked. Nimbus: overview, pipeline list and a first graph. Builder-first, n8n-style canvas. TCO logs and spans policies become two pipelines later. ⚠ Do not promise: a date, or the migration of existing TCO policies.
