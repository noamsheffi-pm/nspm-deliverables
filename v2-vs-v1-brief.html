Usage Metric V2: the usage number moves only when the customer's usage moves.

V2 gives the same unit totals as V1. The difference is that customers can trust it, alert on it, and split it the way they pay for it: CX vs AI, and soon by team and service.

What V2 gives that V1 does not

No false spikes from our deploys

V1Counts the whole day again every minute. A change on our side can double it for a few minutes and fire customer alerts (P0 BUGV2-6332: Conviva, Kotak, Elsevier, Connecteam, Dazn).
V2Counts only what was used in each short window. A change on our side has nothing to recount, so it cannot make a spike.

AI use shown separately from CX use

V1One units number. Olly use is missing, and AI evaluations are counted as spans.
V2AI tokens and AI evaluations are separate. Customers can put AI use on a dashboard or an alert next to their AI quota.

A path to "which team spent it"

V1Frozen: any new label makes a spike. Application and subsystem point to our own exporter, not the customer's workload.
V2Labels can be added safely. Env, type and service labels are next, so customers can see usage per team and service.

Trends and forecasts customers can alert on

V1Resets at midnight and grows all day. Rates, forecasts and anomaly alerts are wrong around midnight (JFrog prediction alerts broke).
V2Each point is real usage, so rates, period-over-period views and forecasts are correct, also across midnight.

Breakdowns that add up

V1Drops series over a cardinality cap with no warning. A breakdown can miss slices while the total looks fine.
V2No silent drop. Any overflow goes to a visible "other" bucket. Adds record counts (logs, spans, events) next to bytes and units.

Fewer holes after our outages

V1If the job is late or down, those minutes are missing for good (Payoneer, dLocal).
V2Events wait in the stream until they are published. Data up to 2 minutes late goes in its own window; later data goes in the next one. Full catch-up after a long outage is not verified yet.

What V2 does not change
  • Billing truth stays the usage reports and the invoice. The metric is for dashboards and alerts. Late data counts in the window it arrives in, so near midnight it can land on the earlier day.
  • Customers rewrite their usage dashboards and alerts once. The metric names and the meaning of a point change. We ship a new dashboard and a guide.
  • Some V1 series are not in V2 yet: the daily quota number, PAYG overage, egress and infra events. Halo and product own closing these before V1 is turned off.
