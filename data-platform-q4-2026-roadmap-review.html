P0BlockedCX-17136 · TCO Audit Events ↗

Know what a pipeline change broke

Show which TCO or routing change cut the data. Show what the next change will break, before it is made.

Why it matters

The largest theme in the corpus. Customers read an empty screen as a bug or lost traffic. Today a TAM writes a DataPrime query to find the truth.

$22.2M
31 accounts ask why their screen is empty or which rule dropped their logs.
14 accounts, $7.8M ask which rule dropped their logs. Razorpay, Kotak, NielsenIQ, BharatPe, JFrog.
DAZN ($2.99M) writes API scripts by hand to answer this.
Miro ($1.90M) used today's tier-impact estimate. It understated the impact: it counts in DataPrime, their alerts use Lucene.
Scope
1 card
Depends on
Radiant (change events) · Phoenix (widget health check)
Success looks like
A customer finds in the product which change cut the data, and when.
Blocked on
Where the change state lives. On Harel's agenda since 27-08.

P2PlanningCX-50821 · Skipped & truncated logs ↗

Full log integrity

Logs over a size limit are cut, or skip parsing, with no notice. Mark every affected log so a query can find it.

Why it matters

Dashboards and alerts stop running on incomplete data without anyone knowing.

~$5.3M
8 accounts: NielsenIQ, Tide, MoneyGram, Kotak, Island, Sony, Lennar, TrustNet.
365scores escalated it as a P1 (COE-877). Every case was found by accident.
Support quoted 32KB, 64KB and 200KB, because the limit is a per-company setting.
Scope
1 card
Depends on
Radiant (marking) · DLI (ingest limits) · Nimbus (UI)
Success looks like
Every cut or skipped log is marked and can be queried.
