What changes

Busy days and quiet days share one total

daily rate × days

One total for the month

Your month total is your daily rate times the days in that month. At 415 a day, November gives you 12,450 units.

up to 3×

Room for a busy day

Any one day can use up to 3 times your daily rate: 1,245 units at the current rate. A new data source or an incident day has room.

From 1 to 8 November you used 1,898 units. That is 15% of the month total after 27% of the days.

Good to know

How the month works in practice

Unused unitsUnits you do not use in a month do not carry over to the next month.
If the month runs outYou get a warning before the month total is used. If it runs out, new data stops until the 1st. Your account manager can add units before that.
One cycleAll teams in your account move to monthly together, including fabric, fabric_audit and fabric-siem. They use the same cycle, from the 1st to the last day of the month.
Your usage viewUsage & Quota still shows usage per day, so you can see which days were busy.
